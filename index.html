USEFUL FROM THE FIRST WINDOW

Make a plan.
Keep it yours.

Free includes one income source, five active bills, two savings buckets, current-window planning, essential corrections, PDFs and local safety backups. Optional Pro adds more plans, calendar-year forecasts, independent scenarios, opt-in widgets and CSV exports. Annual and Lifetime options use localized prices from Apple.

App 1.0 (1) is in production. Apple product setup, final physical QA and the release package remain in progress. No approval, submission or public availability is claimed. TestFlight is skipped by publisher instruction.

Privacy with deliberate choices.

No developer backend, analytics, ads, tracking or bank connection. Optional device authentication covers inactive app content. Financial widget values require Pro and explicit opt-in; they hide for the entire time app lock is enabled and expire until refreshed. Local reminders use generic text.

Manual planning only. PaydayMap does not move money, verify bank settlements or provide accounting, debt, investment, tax or financial advice.
