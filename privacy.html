Your records.
On your device.

PaydayMap is published by Sepideh Javanbakht Dalir. The app has no developer account, backend, analytics, tracking, advertising or bank connection. The app does not send your financial records, notes, device-authentication data or purchase credentials to the developer.

Local records and protection

Income, bills, spending, savings, currencies and preferences are stored locally. Saved libraries, generated exports and the shared widget snapshot are written with Apple’s complete file protection. System and preference storage follows Apple’s operating-system behavior. Optional app lock uses Apple device-owner authentication, including device passcode fallback; the app does not receive a Face ID template or create its own PIN. This is not a claim of custom encrypted banking storage.

Widgets and reminders

The main app and its widget extension share a minimal local App Group snapshot only after Pro and your explicit opt-in. It contains a formatted daily estimate, remainder, days and timestamps, without bill names or transaction history. Values hide while app lock is enabled and expire within an hour or at the next date boundary. Widget availability also depends on Apple’s file-protection and timeline behavior. Optional local reminders use generic wording without amounts or bill names.

Purchases

Apple manages Annual and Lifetime purchases and restores. The app reads verified StoreKit entitlement state to enable Pro. The developer receives no payment-card data or Apple-account password. Apple’s App Store policies apply.

Exports, backups and chosen services

PDF and CSV reports omit private spending notes. A complete local backup includes the records needed to restore your library, including notes. Sharing a file sends it to a destination you choose through Apple’s sheet; that destination’s privacy practices apply. Device backups follow your Apple settings. Protect exported files and keep independent copies where appropriate.

Website and contact

This optional product/support website is hosted by GitHub Pages. Opening it uses your browser and GitHub’s hosting services, which may process ordinary web-request information under their policies. The app does not contact this website in the background. If you email support, your email and information you choose to send are used to answer the request. Do not send private financial backups or credentials.

Your choices

Turn off reminders and widget values in Settings. Disable app lock after device authentication if desired. Spending has recoverable Trash; ledgers and buckets can be archived without erasing history. Removing the app removes its local app-container data; separately exported files and device/cloud backups follow the controls of their storage provider. Contact hello@arvinify.com for privacy questions.
