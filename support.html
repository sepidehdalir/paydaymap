WORKFLOW & SUPPORT

A plan you can
check and correct.

1. Opening cash

Set starting cash for this ledger before the income and spending you will record on the opening date. Earlier cash movements are already part of that starting amount. Exclude savings already set aside. Record each currency separately; there are no exchange-rate conversions. The buffer is reserved once in your current-window estimate.

2. Income and the planning window

Add weekly, every-two-week, twice-monthly, monthly or custom income. Irregular income can be expected or manually received. Expected money is kept separate from cash. The next unpaid expected income ends the default window; choose and Apply a manual end date under Planning dates if needed. Bills on that end date remain reserved.

3. Bills and savings

A planned bill or contribution reserves cash. Open its record to correct an amount, mark it paid/received/contributed, or skip it. Changing the same record avoids a duplicate cash entry. A future-dated paid item stays reserved until its date. A savings target alone does not reserve cash.

4. Repeating plans

Monthly dates clamp to short months without drifting the original anchor. Twice-monthly dates that clamp together produce one item. Revise a future amount without changing recorded history, or stop future repeats after an explicit date. Earlier unpaid items still reserve cash.

5. Spending and refunds

Log everyday spending separately. Mark bill payments in the bill record instead of logging them again. Edit a spending record or move it to Trash. Turn on Show Trash, open the record and Restore spending to recover it. Wait for the app to return to the list after saving before relaunching.

6. PDF and backups

Log → Reports & exports → Current pay-window PDF makes A4 or US Letter output. Save/share it through Apple’s sheet. Settings → Save a local backup preserves the library. Import a validated backup as new archived ledgers; current records stay in place. An unreadable library is preserved and must be exported before explicit recovery replacement. Keep independent backup copies.

7. Pro, restore and expiry

Settings → Upgrade to Pro shows Apple’s actual localized Annual and Lifetime prices. Annual is an auto-renewing yearly subscription; manage/cancel in Apple subscription settings. Lifetime is a one-time purchase and does not cancel an existing Annual subscription. Use Restore Purchases under the same Apple account. Existing saved records remain readable and correctable after Pro ends; new Pro actions require active access.

8. Lock, reminders and widgets

Settings has app lock/widget privacy and local reminders. Device authentication needs a device passcode. Enabling app lock hides all widget financial values, including while the app itself is unlocked. Otherwise values require Pro and an explicit opt-in; they expire within an hour or at the next date boundary until the app refreshes them. Add the small or medium PaydayMap widget through the Home Screen widget gallery. Generic reminders are scheduled for 9 AM in the ledger’s time zone, up to 50 upcoming real-plan items.

Limits of a manual estimate

Cash, due dates and received/paid states depend on your entries. Unpaid overdue commitments carry forward, expected income stays separate and a negative remainder shows a shortfall. Forecast cash includes expected future income and is labeled projected. No account, banking, money movement or financial advice is provided.
